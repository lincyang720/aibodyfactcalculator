## Reusable modelling note
The corridor has three exact, provable constants worth reusing on other pages:
- width = 1.20 × (24.9 − 18.5) = **7.68 points** at every age and for both sexes
- drift = 0.23 × 10 = **2.30 points per decade** (the equation's own age coefficient)
- sex gap = **exactly 10.80 points** at every age and BMI (the −10.8·sex term)
Crossover ages where an age-blind category chart stops making sense: men 49.2 / 52.2 / 82.6;
women 32.7 / 35.7 / 66.1.
