  the exact sum of squared deviations N(N^2-1)/12; the day-count grid (5 scatter levels x 5 weekly rates —
  0.5 kg/wk needs 8/10/14/18/21 days at sigma 0.2/0.3/0.5/0.8/1.0); the smallest detectable weekly rate by
  window (7 days resolves 1.296 kg/wk down to 84 days resolving 0.031) with the total-change-over-window

  weighing = one comparison 60.4 days apart); a 28-day worked series generated here from a stated model
  (true -0.400 kg/wk, sigma 0.5) where regression recovers -0.394 kg/wk (SE 0.071, CI -0.533 to -0.256)
  while the endpoint pair gives -0.156 kg/wk (SE 0.183 — indistinguishable from zero); the **EWMA/SMA
  identity** — at equal variance n = (2-alpha)/alpha and both have mean age (1-alpha)/alpha, so no
  smoother is faster than a plain average of (2-alpha)/alpha days; the graduation table (1 kg display costs
  only 10.1% more days, 0.1 kg costs 0.1%) with the variance share and the sigma^(2/3) scaling; and the
  **100*dW/W ambiguity width** (per kg: 2.00 pts at 50 kg down to 0.91 at 110 kg; 1.28 pts on a 78 kg body),
  which is independent of BF% because the BF terms cancel.
